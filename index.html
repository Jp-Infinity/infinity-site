Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you really close?

In most cases we can close in as little as 14 days from accepting the offer. If you need more time to plan your move, we're flexible — we'll close on your schedule, not ours.

Do I need to make any repairs before selling?

Absolutely not. We buy homes in any condition — roof damage, foundation issues, water damage, mold, fire damage, or simply years of neglect. You don't need to fix, clean, or stage anything. We handle everything after purchase.

Are there really no fees or commissions?

Correct — zero. The offer we make is exactly what you receive at closing. We cover all closing costs. There are no agent commissions, origination fees, or hidden charges of any kind.

How is my offer calculated?

We evaluate your home's current condition, location, comparable sales in the area, and the current market. Our goal is to provide the highest fair cash offer possible. We make money when we resell the home later — so it's in our interest to pay you well and maintain our reputation.

Is my personal information kept private?

100%. We never sell, share, or distribute your information to any third party. Your details are used solely to contact you about your cash offer — that's it.

What if my house is tenant-occupied?

No problem. We purchase tenant-occupied properties regularly and handle all aspects of the transition. You don't need to deal with tenants before closing.

What situations do you help with?

We work with homeowners in all kinds of situations: facing foreclosure, going through divorce, inheriting unwanted property, relocating quickly, dealing with tax liens, or simply wanting to sell fast without the hassle of listing.
